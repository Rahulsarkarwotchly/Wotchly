Our Story

How Wotchly Was Born

It started with a simple frustration: watching a movie with a friend who lives far away, and spending the first ten minutes just trying to press play at the exact same second. Counting down on voice calls. Drifting out of sync after every buffer. Laughing at different moments. The togetherness that made the experience special kept slipping away.

Adrashtra was built to fix that. Not just for movies, but for every shared media experience — anime marathons, live streams, music sessions, documentary binges, and everything in between. Wotchly is our answer: a platform where synchronization is invisible, the chat feels alive, and your friends feel present regardless of where they are in the world.

We believe the best digital experiences are ones that bring people closer, not further apart. Wotchly is proof that the internet, when built thoughtfully, can do exactly that.

Our Vision

A world where physical distance is never a barrier to shared human experiences. We envision a future where watching, listening, and being present together is as natural online as it is in the same room — rich, immediate, and emotionally connected.

We see Wotchly evolving beyond a watch party tool into a universal social layer for all shared media — the place where communities gather, fandoms unite, and friendships are forged through the stories they experience together.

Our Mission

To build the most reliable, accessible, and delightful synchronized media platform on the internet — completely free, for everyone, forever.

We are committed to: zero barriers to entry (no accounts, no fees), privacy-first architecture (collect nothing we don't need), and a platform that works beautifully on any device, in any country, at any time.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Community First

Every decision we make is filtered through one question: does this make the shared experience better? Features, performance, design — all built for the people in the room.

Privacy by Default

We only collect what we absolutely need. No accounts, no tracking, no advertising profiles. Your data is yours. When the room ends, it's gone.

Relentless Reliability

Sync drift, latency spikes, dropped states — these are our enemies. We obsess over millisecond-level precision because even a two-second gap between friends ruins the moment.

Radically Accessible

No app install. No credit card. No minimum system requirements. Wotchly works on any browser, any device — from flagship phones to budget laptops. Everyone deserves to watch together.

Craft & Quality

We care about the details — the smoothness of animations, the precision of micro-interactions, the clarity of the interface under stress. Good enough is never good enough.

Built with Love

Wotchly is a passion project at its core. We build it because we use it. Every feature that ships is something we genuinely wanted and built for ourselves first.
