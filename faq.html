Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: July 2025

What is Wotchly?
Wotchly is a free, real-time synchronized watch-party platform built by Adrashtra. It lets you watch videos, movies, anime, and streams together with friends anywhere in the world — in perfect sync. When the host plays, pauses, or seeks, everyone in the room experiences it simultaneously. No lag, no drift.
Who made Wotchly?
Wotchly is built and maintained by Adrashtra — a technology company passionate about building connected, social digital experiences. Our mission is to bring people closer together through shared moments, starting with the universal language of entertainment.
Do I need an account?
Absolutely not. No email, no password, no sign-up form. Just choose a display name and you're instantly in the room. Your name is saved locally in your browser so you don't have to type it every time.
How many people can join a room?
Each room supports up to 50 concurrent viewers. For larger events or communities, consider creating multiple synchronized rooms with shared IMDb/TMDB IDs so everyone stays in sync.
What video sources are supported?
Wotchly supports a wide range of sources: YouTube, direct MP4/WebM/MKV links, HLS streams (.m3u8), DASH streams (.mpd), Google Drive video links, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Spotify, SoundCloud, JioSaavn, Gaana, and general website URLs for co-browsing. The Stream Embed Engine also supports IMDb IDs, TMDB IDs, Netflix URLs, Prime Video URLs, Hotstar URLs, Disney+ URLs, and anime search via AniList.
How do I watch Netflix / Prime / Hotstar with friends?
Use the Stream Embed Engine in the Load Media modal. You can either paste the show's Netflix/Prime/Hotstar URL directly, or find the IMDb ID of the movie/show (e.g., tt0944947 for Game of Thrones) and paste it in. Then select the content type (Movie or TV Show), enter the season and episode if applicable, and click Load Embed. If one provider doesn't work, try switching to a different one in the Provider dropdown.
Why does the stream say "loading" but not play?
Third-party embed providers can sometimes be slow or temporarily unavailable. Try these steps: (1) Switch to a different provider in the Provider dropdown — try VidSrc TO, then Embed SU, then VidLink Pro. (2) Make sure you're entering a valid IMDb ID (starts with tt) or TMDB numeric ID. (3) Check if the content is available in your region — some titles may be geo-restricted. (4) Try a different browser or disable ad blockers, as some providers block requests from ad-blocked environments.
How do I watch anime with Crunchyroll?
Crunchyroll uses DRM (digital rights management) that prevents direct embedding. However, you can search for anime using the Anime / Crunchyroll tab in the Stream Embed section. Type the anime name, select it from the results, and choose an episode. It will stream via the selected provider using TMDB/AniList data for the best match.
How does HLS / m3u8 streaming work?
Paste any .m3u8 URL into the media input and click Load. Wotchly uses HLS.js to decode and play the stream natively in your browser with adaptive bitrate support. This works for IPTV streams, live broadcasts, and on-demand HLS content. The audio language selector will automatically appear if multiple audio tracks are detected.
Can I use Google Drive links?
Yes! Paste a Google Drive share link (e.g., https://drive.google.com/file/d/...) and Wotchly will automatically extract the file ID and load it as a native video player. Make sure the sharing is set to "Anyone with the link can view" in Google Drive settings.
Who controls playback?
The room Host — the person who created the room — controls all playback. Play, pause, seek, quality changes, and media loading are Host-only actions. All other viewers' players automatically sync to the host. Hosts can transfer host status to any other user via the Settings → Host Controls panel.
How do I share a room?
Tap the Share button (the icon next to the room code in the header) or tap the room code chip itself. This copies a direct invite link to your clipboard. Send it to anyone — when they open the link, they'll see a join screen to enter their display name before jumping in.
What are voice notes?
Voice notes let you record and send a short audio message in the room chat. Tap the microphone icon in the chat input bar, hold to record, release to send. You can also upload an existing audio file using the upload icon. Voice notes are stored temporarily in the room session and are deleted when the session ends.
Can I use Wotchly on mobile?
Absolutely — Wotchly is designed mobile-first. It's a Progressive Web App (PWA): open it in your mobile browser, tap "Add to Home Screen" for a native app experience on both iOS (Safari) and Android (Chrome). It supports landscape mode, background audio, and media session controls on lock screen.
Does audio keep playing when I switch apps?
Yes. Wotchly has background audio support and integrates with the system media session API, so playback continues when you switch apps or lock your screen. Playback controls appear on your lock screen and notification shade. When you return to the app, it automatically resyncs to the host's current position.
What is Landscape / Force Landscape mode?
On mobile, the landscape icon in the control bar forces the player into landscape mode (rotated view) even without physically rotating your device. This is useful on phones that don't auto-rotate. If your device is already in landscape (rotated), Wotchly automatically switches to a side-by-side layout with the video on the left and chat on the right.
What equalizer and audio settings are available?
Open Settings → Audio. You can adjust: Master Volume, Bass, Treble, and Stereo Balance. There are also 8 equalizer presets: Normal, Bass Boost, Treble Boost, Vocal Clarity, Rock, Jazz, Classical, and Electronic. Settings are saved to your device for future sessions.
Can the host lock a room?
Yes. Hosts can lock a room from Settings → Host Controls. When a room is locked, new users cannot join. The host can unlock the room at any time. Hosts can also kick specific users or transfer host privileges to any viewer in the room.
Is Wotchly free?
Completely free — no subscriptions, no premium tiers, no paywalls. Wotchly may display non-intrusive ads to help cover server and infrastructure costs. There are no plans to introduce any paid features.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
Visit our Contact page. You can report bugs or submit feature requests via email — we read every message and actively use feedback to shape future development.
